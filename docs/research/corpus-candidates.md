# Starter corpus candidates for issue #8

**Checked 2026-11-04.** No verified public corpus found here covers all four languages, source-PDF modes, DOCX-export engineering graphics, formulas with exact math truth, and signatures under one unrestricted rights grant. Keep the core small: licensed DocLayNet pages plus original eOCR fixtures. The other public leads below are separate or conditional, not cleared core payloads.

## Public-source matrix

| Source and exact rights | Coverage and page annotations | Can we select pages without bulk download? | Selection recommendation |

Its provider represents it obtained the right to publish the data. | 80,863 human-annotated pages from financial reports, scientific articles, law, tenders, manuals and patents. English appears in examples; the owner gives no language breakdown. Formula annotations are regions, not equation transcription. ([owner README](https://github.com/DS4SD/DocLayNet/blob/main/README.md), [class/schema listing](https://huggingface.co/datasets/docling-project/DocLayNet-v1.1)) | **Partial:** the HF viewer exposes individual page rows (useful for previewing/selecting PNG and COCO annotations), but the underlying release is in ~1 GiB Parquet shards. ([HF viewer and files](https://huggingface.co/datasets/docling-project/DocLayNet-v1.1/tree/main/data), [owner README](https://github.com/DS4SD/DocLayNet/blob/main/README.md)) | **Public anchor.** Pin a modest manual/patent/science sample with picture and formula regions. The card says the sources include digital collections but does not list item-level image rights. | **Yes for an image:** page images are individual files in the HF tree, while annotations are in a shared `label.csv`; no per-page annotation file is listed. ([image tree](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ByteDance/AncientDoc/tree/main/imgs/imgs)) | **Conditional lead, not cleared core data.** Check source-item rights before redistribution; useful only as a historical Traditional Chinese scan stress sample, not contemporary Chinese documents. |

## Restricted or unresolved leads (exclude from core payload)

- **XFUND-ZH:** [official release](https://github.com/doc-analysis/XFUND/releases/tag/v1.0) / [README](https://github.com/doc-analysis/XFUND) labels Chinese `ZH`, provides image forms with JSON text, boxes, entity labels and links ([reader format](https://github.com/microsoft/unilm/blob/master/layoutlmft/layoutlmft/data/datasets/xfun.py)), and licenses project content **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**. It does not declare Simplified vs Traditional. Images are in split archives, with no per-image download documented. Keep it only as a separately licensed, non-commercial evaluation slice; do not mix into a commercial or permissively redistributed corpus.
- **FUNSD:** official [terms](https://guillaumejaume.github.io/FUNSD/work/) restrict use to non-commercial research/education and leave copyright clearance for its real scanned images to the user. Its official download is a dataset archive, not individual pages. Exclude from payload absent separate image clearance.
- **Russian PDFs:** [HumynLabs card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HumynLabs/Russian_Documents_Dataset_PDF) declares **CC BY 4.0**, but names only broad source categories, not item-level rights/provenance; it has PDFs without page annotations. The HF tree exposes individual files, but individual access does not resolve rights. Defer pending source audit; use synthetic Russian fixtures meanwhile.

**Practical minimum:** if bulk transfer is acceptable, sample 20–30 DocLayNet pages for real digital layout, then create 2–3 eOCR-authored multipage documents spanning English, Russian, Simplified/Traditional Chinese, digital/scanned/mixed PDF, graphics, formulas and signatures. For a no-bulk seed, use generated fixtures and optionally preview individual DocLayNet rows; its source PDFs/text sidecars have no documented per-page download. AncientDoc images are individually listed, but remain conditional on source-item rights review.
